## Scope

Whole marketing site (landing first, then bundles, agents, about, case study, FAQ, demo, legal, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade. Local build only on branch `redesign`; not deployed.

## Audience and job

Anyone working in insurance, often a staff member who will recommend it upward, and the heads of larger firms (never named or addressed). Must believe in ten seconds: a full AI team that does real work, safely and under their control. Action: book a 20-minute demo, or WhatsApp.

## Proof and constraints

Real: built and run inside Brocare Insurance Brokerage, Beirut; 24 agents with real outputs; two-week guarantee; approval before anything goes out. No counts, clients or testimonials exist; demonstration entries are labelled as an example. Fixed blue palette, one gradient word per title, no em dashes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a live insurance placing slip. Each agent writes and initials its own line; the final box is always the visitor's to approve. Refuses the category default of a 3D robot hero over glass feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Navy form stock (#05081A ground, #0A0F2E and #0D1440 sheets) ruled in #1E3A6E hairlines with #1A3BDB section rules; field labels in small condensed caps; entries set in a mono as if typed; round rubber agent stamps in #2D6FFF with ink texture; ice #4FC3F7 only where something is live or approved. Numbered schedule sections, reference codes, tick boxes, sign-here lines. Archivo (width axis) plus Martian Mono.

STORY: Visitor sees the team at work on one document, understands every line is an agent's output waiting for a human, trusts the control and the two-week warranty, then signs: books the demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width schedule sheet. Top strip: form code and sheet number. Left two thirds: headline set monumental in the Insured field. Right third and below: example agent lines type in one by one, each stamped with initials. Bottom row: an empty Approved by box where a stamp follows the pointer and presses on click, the Book a demo button beside it.

FORM: The Placing Slip, fourth on the ordered grounded list; seed key 78696e33. Signature interaction: the approval stamp. Motion grammar: rules draw, entries type, stamps press.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
